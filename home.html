Our Story

These are the recipes of Rachel Marez Chapman. My Nonnie was originally from New Mexico where the Marez name is very well establsihed. The Marez family name belongs to that landscape in a way that feels inseparable. Generations of New Mexican history are preserved here, where life was built slowly and faithfully through land, labor, and community.

Many of these recipes were written old index cards, passed along by memory, or saved from recipes found on miscellaneous boxes. They are not just instructions, but pieces of a her life shared between generations, holidays celebrated, and everyday meals made special. Everyone knows that anything that was made by her, no matter how crazy the ingredients were, carried the feeling of home, as if love itself had been intentionally made into every dish.

This archive is my way of preserving those memories and sharing them, so that her cooking, her traditions, and a little bit of her love can continue to live on.
